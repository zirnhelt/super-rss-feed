Free-text journal for the weekly calibration agent. Each run appends a dated

## Standing lessons

_Folded from journal entries through 2026-09-06; rewritten each fold, not appended._

- Quality gate: raising `min_claude_score` is the only lever that has demonstrably improved user-rated quality. Moves: 20→13 (June 24, loosened deliberately), then 13→16→18→20→23 through July–August. Each raise cut a larger share of user-rated bad articles at a modest cost to good ones; the sweep through 25 remains favorable. Treat ~23–25 as the working range, and re-check the sweep before going higher.
- Volume is not the constraint. Final feeds run 400–800 per run against a ~100 target, so tightening the floor costs no usable output. Do not loosen knobs to chase volume.
- Composite scoring lacks discriminatory power: even the 80–100 band is only ~57–58% good, and the 40–59 band is near coin-flip. Gains from threshold tuning are capped until the composite weighting is fixed.
- Structural cause (not tunable here): the local dimension is a binary cliff (~99% of non-regional articles score 0–19), so L=0 dominates the 0.25Q/0.55R/0.20L composite for most content. Relevance is also collapsed for most categories. Weighting and prompt changes are human-only.
- Weekday theme-score collapse (late July to Aug 10) was resolved by the August 11 dimensional rearchitecture. Keep monitoring weekday `mean_theme_score_raw`; a recurrence should be escalated to a human, not patched with min_score changes.
- Podcast theme misses (~20–28% correction rate, mostly theme-scoring rather than routing) need human prompt review. Do not adjust theme keywords automatically.
- Category pattern to watch: news and scifi have high bad rates; homestead and climate perform well. Per-category thresholds are not yet justified by sample size.
- Standing directive: do not raise `feed_slots` or loosen `calibration_bounds` without evidence that article counts are consistently under ~120/day. This condition is not currently met.
- Open question: whether the recent upward drift in noise-to-signal (late August–early September) reflects the min_claude_score raise biting or an upstream content-mix shift. Resolve before any further floor change.
- Open question: how much of the remaining bad rate is recoverable by quality-gate tuning alone versus requiring source-level blocking (human-only; several worst sources already listed as done).
