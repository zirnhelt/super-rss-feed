Product reviews and buying guides: a review of one consumer product (phone, headphones, TV, car, charger), "best X" guides, ranked lists of products or destinations, price-drop posts. KEEP hands-on technical depth: teardowns, repairs, builds, measured testing.
US domestic politics (the United States only, never Canada): partisan politics, Congress and White House fights, US political figures, immigration enforcement, and US health-policy or culture-war battles (vaccine politics, Medicare/Medicaid administration). KEEP a US story whose primary subject applies beyond the US (how AI or platforms can be regulated at all, including a US leader's stance on it; US science agencies and research funding such as NIH, NASA or NOAA; a scientific finding), that has a direct Canadian effect (tariffs, softwood lumber, the border, shared water), or in which a Canadian government or leader is a principal actor.
Routine mining and commodity-market items: junior-miner financings and private placements, drill-hole results, sponsored posts, sales and takeovers of mines outside Canada, and daily price or market-close updates. KEEP mining and farming stories about policy, permits, land use, First Nations agreements or public investment in Canada, any project in BC, and industry-wide shifts (supply chains, new technology, market structure).
Police blotter and crashes: RCMP and police releases, arrests, charges, court appearances, sentencings and appeals in individual criminal cases, drug and weapons seizures, police safety tips, border-enforcement tallies, vehicle collisions and crash reports. KEEP court rulings or inquiries that change law or policy (Gladue, Indigenous title, drug law), policing budgets and policy debates, wildfire and emergency operations (including arson, evacuations and search and rescue), election-campaign coverage including a candidate's record, and any case that charges a sitting elected official, whatever the season.
Video games: game releases, reviews, studios, developers and game-industry business, consoles, gaming PCs and accessories, esports. KEEP hacking, repair, emulation or preservation of game hardware and software.

# Proposed 2026-09-27 from 37 rating notes — merge to adopt, close to decline.
Listicles and ranked lists (non-product): generic 'best of' or numbered lists of books, towns, articles, or cultural works without substantial reporting or analysis. KEEP lists with reporting, interviews, or original research; KEEP ranked lists of products (already covered under product reviews).
Opinion columns and signed opinion pieces: editorials, columns, and op-eds expressing a writer's viewpoint on a subject. KEEP news reporting on the subject itself, even if it quotes opinions; KEEP columns by Cariboo writers or officials, or about the Cariboo.

# Proposed 2026-10-04 from 89 rating notes — merge to adopt, close to decline.
Doomsaying and catastrophizing: alarmist framing of speculative risks without proportional analysis, existential panic takes on AI, technology, or environmental scenarios. KEEP reporting on specific incidents, policy changes, research findings, or expert analysis with concrete evidence.
Ragebait and inflammatory takes: articles designed primarily to provoke outrage through divisive framing or inflammatory headlines rather than substantive analysis. KEEP reporting on genuine controversies with balanced treatment of claims and evidence.
Bad news without local relevance or actionable context: articles about crimes, accidents, or disasters reported elsewhere with no connection to the reader's region and no reporting depth that distinguishes them from wire-service briefs. KEEP local crime/accident reporting; KEEP international stories with substantial investigation or analysis.

Gardening and home-maintenance how-tos: seasonal planting tips, lawn and rose care, and gardens of famous people. KEEP policy or research coverage of agriculture and land use.

Warfare and military technology, with no exception: wars and armed conflicts anywhere (battles, strikes, offensives, casualties, ceasefires and peace talks), military operations and deployments, weapons and munitions, military drones and drone warfare, defence industry, startups and procurement, defence budgets and arms deals.
